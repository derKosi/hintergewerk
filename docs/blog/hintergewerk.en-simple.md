# The Memory of a Wall

*Simple English · November 18, 2026*

## What this is about

Sometimes a wall is open.
Then you can see the pipes and cables inside the wall.
Later, the wall gets closed.
Then nobody can see the pipes anymore.

This is dangerous.
Here is an example:
Later, someone drills a hole into the wall.
The drill hits a pipe.
Water runs into the wall.
Or there is a short circuit.

So we need a memory for the wall.
A memory here means:
The app remembers the pipes and cables.
Even many months later.

## What is Hintergewerk?

Hintergewerk is the name of our app.
The app runs on a VR headset.
A VR headset is glasses that show you a digital world.
In this project, the headset is called Meta Quest.

## How the app works

A worker opens a wall.

**Step 1: Draw the pipe.**
The worker points a finger at the wall.
They pinch their fingers together.
Then they draw a line on the wall.
The line shows: here is the pipe. Here is the cable.

**Step 2: Say how deep it is.**
The worker moves two hands apart.
The app shows: the pipe is this deep behind the wall.

**Step 3: Take a photo.**
The worker takes a photo.
The photo shows: what did the pipe look like?

**Step 4: Save.**
The worker pinches again.
The app saves the pipe.
The app now knows:
- Where is the pipe?
- What is it? For example: water or power?
- How deep is it?
- Who saved it?
- When was it saved?

And in the other hand, the worker holds a plan.
The plan shows the room from above.
Every new pipe appears on the plan right away.

## Many months later

Someone works on the wall again.
This person wears the VR headset.
The app shows the pipes from before.
Not as a thin line.
As a thick, glowing band.
The band is about 5 centimeters wide.

Does a hand come close to the band?
Then the app makes a sound.
And the band turns into a warning color.
So the person knows: do not drill here.

## One important rule

Our app can NOT see through the wall.
The app only remembers what was saved before.

For every pipe, the app shows:
Was it measured exactly?
Or is the position only an estimate?
This is always written down.

And this too:
The app shows nothing?
That does NOT mean: the wall is empty.
It only means: there is no saved information for this spot.

## What else the app can do

The app can print a floor plan.
As a PDF.
The app can also create a data file.
Experts call it JSON.
Other computer programs can use this file.

## Who is the app for?

- People who build and renovate.
- Companies.
- Homeowners.
- The next company that works on the wall later.

## What comes later?

The app should do more in the future.
For example:
- Record whole houses. Not just one room.
- Collect scanner data. A scanner is a measuring device for rooms.
- Make suggestions. For example: where is a good place for a new cable?

## Our wish

A wall should be able to remember.
Then nobody drills into a pipe by accident.
