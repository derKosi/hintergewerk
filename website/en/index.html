Hintergewerk

The work behind the surface: what your walls once showed stays findable — spatially anchored, with depth, type, timestamp and installer.

„Hintergewerk" is German for *the work behind* — because the important stuff hides in the wall.

The problem

Every wall tells the truth exactly once: while it's open. Then the knowledge lives in a few scattered photos — or nowhere at all. Until a drill meets a pipe nobody remembered.

How it works

1 · CaptureWall is open? Trace the run with your hand, set depth with a two-hand gesture, attach a photo. Under 30 seconds.
2 · RecallMonths later the app brings the wall's memory back — as a safety corridor instead of a thin line. Your hand approaches, the warning escalates.
3 · Hand overOne gesture exports a floor plan as PDF with legend and scale — the document the homeowner accepts and the next firm inherits.

Our rule

We do not claim to see through walls. We remember what was documented.
"Not found" never means "not there".

What's inside

Hands-firstFive gestures — pinch, two-hand distance, palm, swipe, gaze+pinch. No controller, no keyboard.
Spatially anchoredSpatial anchors keep the memory in place — across app restarts and weeks.
Safety corridorNo hairline that lies: ±5 cm shown honestly, with an escalating warning on approach.
Evidence includedPhoto, depth, confidence, installer, timestamp — every object carries its provenance.

Who it's for

  • Electricians & plumbers — find your own runs in the client's building
  • DIY renovators — no nasty surprises at the next drill
  • Property managers & shops — a documented stock instead of tribal knowledge
