Hintergewerk

We are building a program for VR glasses. The program remembers: what is inside your walls?

Say it like this: HIN-ter-guh-verk. It means: the work behind.

The problem

Sometimes a wall is open. Then you can see pipes and cables inside.

Later, the wall is closed. Then you cannot see the pipes anymore.

This is dangerous. For example: Someone drills into the wall. The drill hits a pipe. Water comes out. Or there is a short circuit.

So we need a memory for the wall.

How it works

Step 1 · Write it downThe wall is open. You draw with your hand in the air. The drawing sits right at the pipe. You take a photo. It takes less than 30 seconds.
Step 2 · RememberLater you come back to the wall. The glasses show the drawing. The drawing is wide and glowing. You see clearly: here is a pipe. Please do not drill here!
3 · A sheet for everyoneYou can print a plan. The plan shows all pipes and cables. Other people can read the plan.

Our rule

We do not see through the wall.
We only remember: what was written down before?

What can the program do?

You only use your handsYou do not need a controller. Hands are enough.
The drawing staysYou turn the glasses off. You turn them on again. The drawing is still there.
The drawing warns youThe drawing is wide. Your hand comes close. Then it glows red.
There is proofFor every pipe: a photo. And: who wrote it down? When?

Who is it for?

  • For electricians and plumbers
  • For people who fix their own homes
  • For companies with many buildings
